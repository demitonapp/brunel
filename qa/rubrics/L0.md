# L0 rubric — SCRAPPY

The tier checklist *is* the rubric. Score a render, never a script.

Each item is tagged **hard** (machine-checkable or unambiguous to the eye) or **soft** (judgement).
The critic proposes; the human decides.

| # | Criterion | Kind | Checked by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One `.blend` per episode, ≤10 assets, procedural or flat colour, no external textures | hard | `manifest.json` part count |
| 2 | 6–8 shots per 72 s (3–6 shots/minute) | hard | `harness validate` |
| 3 | Every shot static or a single move; no move with BOTH translation and zoom | hard | spec inspection |
| 4 | Exactly one sun/HDRI plus one fill; no light linking | hard | spec inspection |
| 5 | Principled BSDF with base colour + roughness only; no normal maps | hard | material audit |
| 6 | 1 BU = 1 m; hero dimensions within ±10% of a cited source | **hard** | `harness build` assertions |
| 7 | ≥1 dead frame per 10 s | soft | frame-by-frame contact sheet |
| 8 | Cuts land off the VO beat | soft | animatic review |
| 9 | Audio = 2 layers (VO + one music bed); zero SFX | hard | stem count |
| 10 | Captions burned in, may sit in the bottom 350 px | hard | frame inspection |
| 11 | 1080×1920, ≤90 s, H.264, under 50 MB | hard | `ffprobe` on the deliverable |
| 12 | ≥1 primary source cited in the description | hard | `factgate` + caption text |

## Promotion rule

Exactly **three** items move from failing to passing per episode. Twelve episodes, thirty-six
promoted items: that is the whole compounding mechanism made countable.

## Anti-gaming rule

Do not "fix" a failing item by removing the thing that exposes it. Deleting the scale figure so
that criterion 6 has nothing to measure is not a pass.
